Six Octobers of Sentinel-2 true colour (2021-10-24, 2022-10-19, 2023-10-29, 2024-10-28, 2025-10-23, 2026-10-23, every one under 1.5% cloud and covering the whole box) are in the timelapse, and the box-mean NDWI fell from 0.45 (2021-10-24) to 0.35 (2026-10-23), a change of -0.10, which is a direction, not a shoreline position. Step through the frames to look at the island edge directly; I used my own 9 km box on the cape, not round 4's 33 km one. A 10 m index averaged over a box mixes open water, sound, wet sand and vegetation, so tide, turbidity or sand moisture can move it as much as land does, and I looked up no tide stage for any scene time. Six frames are one scene a year, not a trend: they cannot say whether a lower value is erosion, a different tide, or a storm week.

Learning → The recommended scene is just an id, so "first item" can be the wrong tile. `recommended` returns the id, coverage, cloud and reason only. My first pass looked the item up by a key the dict does not have and took the first item in the list, which in 2021 was tile 18SVD at 7.7% cloud, not the recommended 18SVE at 0.0%. Building the layer from the recommended id fixed it; the layer needs no bbox, but then each frame re-fetches its STAC item.
