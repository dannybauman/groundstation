THE FIELD TEST №22

groundstation · 5 prompts · 5 live artifacts · 3 works, 2 partly, 0 not yet · October 30, 2026 · Development Seed labs

UK EO DataHub's open catalog (CEDA's STAC of UK Sentinel ARD and EOCIS products) is now a fourth catalog, beside Earth Search, NASA VEDA and Planetary Computer. Its pixels are public COGs, so the shared tiler draws them with no account. These 5 asks cover what that opens: a UK optical swipe, band math inside a packed 10-band file, radar, a class map and a scaled vegetation product. Not here yet: EODH's commercial imagery (an account), its own tiler (an account) and its NetCDF products (UKCP18, CMIP6, land surface temperature, sea ice), which this render path cannot draw. Run on add-eodh-catalog at 0.69.0.

Learning → Works, with a label to ignore and clouds to look for. The expression names nir and red, which are bands inside one file here, not assets. groundstation reads the item's eo:bands and turns them into band 7 and band 3 of that file. The tiler then labels the result (cog_Red-cog_Red)/(cog_Red+cog_Red). That label is wrong and the number is right: the file names its bands Blue, Green, Red, RE_B5, RE_B6, RE_B7, NIR_B8, and a woodland pixel read directly gives red 49, NIR 320, an NDVI of 0.73.
